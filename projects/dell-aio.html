Standalone PC

Turning a Dell Inspiron 5400 all-in-one motherboard into a standalone machine without the original internal display.

Dell IPTGL-CL11th Gen IntelHDMIMotherboard repair

Project overview

I wanted to reuse the motherboard from a broken all-in-one instead of letting the whole machine become e-waste. The goal was simple: make the board boot and work as a normal compact PC while using an external HDMI display.

The interesting parts were the power-button wiring, fan control, display troubleshooting, and understanding the board's display-detection path.

Key points

Standalone power-button connection.
CPU fan wiring with tach + PWM.
HDMI output troubleshooting.
Boardview / schematic-guided repair.
