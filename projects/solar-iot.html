Solar IoT
Model

A compact solar-powered control experiment built around an ESP32, a small panel, a 5 V battery pack, and isolated relay switching.

ESP32-WROOM5 V / 1 A panelRelayWeChat mini-program

Project overview

The system explores a tiny solar charging and remote-control setup: a panel charges a sealed 5 V battery pack, while an ESP32 handles control and telemetry. A relay provides isolation for the output load.

Hardware choices will keep evolving as the prototype is tested in real conditions.

Planned features

Solar charging path.
ESP32 telemetry and control.
Relay-isolated output.
Remote control from a small web/mobile UI.
