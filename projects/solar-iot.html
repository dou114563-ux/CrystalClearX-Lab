Solar IoT
Model

A compact solar-powered control experiment built around an ESP32, battery pack, and isolated relay switching.

ESP32-WROOM5V 1A panelRelay isolationWeChat mini-program

Project overview

The model combines a 5V solar panel with a sealed 5V battery pack and ESP32 control. A relay provides isolation between the controller and the output load, while the software side is designed around simple remote control.

Key components

ESP32-WROOM controller.
5V 1A solar panel.
5V battery pack / DC input.
Relay for electrical isolation.
Protection diode and controlled output.
