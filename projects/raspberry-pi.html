Project overview

The Pi acts as a tiny always-on lab machine: DNS filtering, local services, remote access experiments, and automation all live in one low-power box.

The fun part is that every service is another excuse to learn something about Linux, networking, proxies, containers, or Cloudflare.

What lives here

Pi-hole / DNS filtering.
Self-hosted web and bot experiments.
Network and TUN/proxy experiments.
Activity recording and diagnostics.
